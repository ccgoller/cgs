## 3. CV / Résumé Building Blocks

Everything you do in this course can go on your CV. Track your accomplishments below.

| Category | Item | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Coursework | Genetics / Microbiology Teaching Lab | Semester/Year |
| Technical skills | List techniques mastered (see Skills Inventory) | |
| Presentations | Lab section data presentation | |
| Awards / recognition | Dean's List, departmental award, etc. | |
| Volunteer / service | Lab prep volunteer, tutoring, outreach | |

**CV Tips:**
- Use **action verbs**: *cultivated, analysed, designed, presented, collaborated*.
- Quantify where possible: "Performed 50+ serial dilution plating assays."
- Tailor your CV to each opportunity — highlight the most relevant experience.
- Have a faculty mentor or the **university writing centre** review your CV.

## 4. Networking and Mentorship

### Finding a Mentor

- Attend **departmental seminars** and introduce yourself to speakers.
- Visit faculty office hours — not just when you need something.
- Ask your TA about their research; many TAs can connect you to their PI.
- Join the **Biology / Microbiology student association** on your campus.

### Informational Interviews

Reach out to graduate students, postdocs, or industry scientists for a 20-min conversation. Template email:

> Subject: Brief informational chat request — [Your Name], undergraduate researcher  
>
> Dear [Name],  
> I am a [year] undergraduate at NC State studying [major]. I am exploring career paths in [field] and would love to hear about your experience in [their role/area]. Would you be available for a 20-minute video call or coffee chat in the next few weeks? I am happy to work around your schedule.  
> Thank you for your time,  
> [Your Name]

## 5. Applying to Research Opportunities

### On-Campus Research

- Search **NC State's undergraduate research office** for faculty positions.
- Email PIs whose work interests you with a brief, professional inquiry.
- Apply to **work-study positions** in labs if financial support is needed.

### Summer Programs (REUs and Equivalents)

| Program Type | Where to Find | Deadline Window |
|---|---|---|
| NSF REU (Research Experiences for Undergraduates) | [nsf.gov/crssprgm/reu](https://www.nsf.gov/crssprgm/reu/) | Oct – Feb |
| NIH Summer Internship (SIP) | [training.nih.gov](https://www.training.nih.gov/) | Nov – Mar |
| Industry internships | Company career pages, Handshake | Rolling |
| NOAA, CDC, USDA programs | Agency websites | Varies |

> ⭐ **Apply broadly and early.** Competition is high and deadlines can be in October.

## 6. Graduate School Preparation

If you are considering an M.S. or Ph.D., start planning in your **junior year**.

### Timeline

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Freshman / Sophomore | Build GPA; get into a research lab; attend seminars |
| Junior year (fall) | Identify target programs; prepare for GRE (if required) |
| Junior year (spring) | Ask professors for recommendation letters |
| Senior year (fall) | Write personal statement; submit applications (Oct–Dec) |
| Senior year (spring) | Interview at graduate programs; make decision |

### Personal Statement Tips

- Tell a **coherent scientific story** — how did you arrive here?
- Be specific: name professors you want to work with and why.
- Explain gaps or weaknesses proactively and briefly.
- Have at least three people review your draft.

### Asking for Recommendation Letters

1. Ask **at least 6–8 weeks** before the deadline.
2. Provide: your CV, personal statement draft, list of programs, and deadlines.
3. Send a thank-you note after letters are submitted.

## 7. Exploring Non-Academic Careers

A biology degree opens many doors beyond academia.

| Career Path | Example Roles | Key Skills to Highlight |
|---|---|---|
| Biotechnology / Pharma | Research associate, QC analyst | Lab techniques, GLP/GMP awareness |
| Science communication | Science writer, outreach coordinator | Writing, public speaking |
| Public health | Epidemiologist (with additional training), lab tech | Microbiology, data analysis |
| Science policy | Legislative aide, regulatory affairs | Writing, policy literacy |
| Medicine / Pharmacy | MD, PharmD (professional school) | Strong GPA, research, volunteering |
| Teaching | K-12 science teacher, community college instructor | Communication, patience |
| Entrepreneurship | Biotech startup | All of the above + business basics |
