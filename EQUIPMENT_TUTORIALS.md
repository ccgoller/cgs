# 🧪 Equipment Tutorials

Simple, safety-first guides for undergraduate researchers and teaching assistants.

> Before using any instrument independently, complete lab safety training and check in with your TA or instructor.

---

## 1. Implen NP80 Nano Spectrophotometer

### Safe use
1. Wear gloves and wipe gloves if they contact samples.
2. Keep the pedestal clean and dry to avoid bad readings and corrosion.
3. Never force the arm closed.

### Quick steps
1. Turn on the instrument and open the NP80 software.
2. Select the correct method (for example DNA, RNA, or protein).
3. Clean top and bottom pedestals with lint-free wipe + DI water, then dry.
4. Blank with buffer used in your samples.
5. Load 1–2 µL sample, lower arm gently, and read.
6. Wipe pedestals immediately after each sample.

### Care checklist
- Clean pedestals before and after every run.
- Do not leave sample residue to dry on the optical surface.
- Shut down software properly before power off.

## 2. Cerillo Bio (Alto) Plate Reader

### Safe use
1. Only use sealed, clean plates with no liquid on the outside.
2. Keep fingers away from moving tray parts.
3. Do not move the reader while it is running.

### Quick steps
1. Power on and open the Cerillo control software.
2. Choose the correct protocol (OD, kinetics, endpoint, etc.).
3. Check plate orientation (A1 corner matches software map).
4. Insert plate gently and start run.
5. Export data to the assigned lab folder.

### Care checklist
- Wipe spills immediately with approved lab wipes.
- Keep tray and plate path free of dust and tape residue.
- Report unusual noise or tray movement right away.

## 3. Invitrogen Fluorometer (Qubit 4.0)

### Safe use
1. Use only Qubit assay tubes (not standard microcentrifuge tubes).
2. Protect dyes from light and wear eye/skin protection.
3. Mix gently; avoid bubbles that can affect fluorescence.

### Quick steps
1. Turn on Qubit 4.0 and select the assay kit (DNA, RNA, or protein).
2. Prepare standards and samples exactly as the kit specifies.
3. Vortex 2–3 seconds, then incubate for required time.
4. Calibrate using the two standards.
5. Read samples and record concentration with units.

### Care checklist
- Keep assay tubes clean on the outside before reading.
- Cap reagent bottles immediately after use.
- Do not reuse assay tubes for new measurements.

## 4. BioTek Synergy HT Plate Reader

### Safe use
1. Verify plate type matches method (96-well, clear/black/white plate).
2. Keep lid closed during reads unless protocol says otherwise.
3. Use proper PPE when handling biohazard or chemical plates.

### Quick steps
1. Open Gen5 software and load approved lab protocol.
2. Confirm wavelength/filter and read mode.
3. Insert correctly oriented plate.
4. Run plate and monitor for error messages.
5. Save raw data and exported table immediately.

### Care checklist
- Remove plates promptly after run completion.
- Clean external spills only with approved cleaner.
- Log any lamp/filter warnings in instrument log.

---

## 5. LogPhase 600 Plate Reader

### Safe use
1. Ensure plate bottom is clean and free of condensation.
2. Do not open or touch internal optics.
3. Keep power and USB cables secure to prevent sudden disconnects.

### Quick steps
1. Launch the LogPhase 600 software and connect instrument.
2. Select pre-set protocol for your experiment.
3. Load plate with correct orientation and start run.
4. Confirm run completes and export data.
5. Back up data to shared course folder.

### Care checklist
- Clean tray area after each lab section.
- Never stack objects on top of the instrument.
- Report repeated read errors before the next class.

---

## 6. USA Scientific Device (Foral Bioruptor)

### Safe use
1. Sonication can generate heat and aerosols—use closed tubes and recommended tube holders.
2. Use hearing protection if your local SOP requires it.
3. Keep water level and cooling conditions within manufacturer guidance.

### Quick steps
1. Inspect tube holder, water bath, and power connections.
2. Add samples in balanced positions.
3. Set cycle parameters (ON/OFF time and total cycles) from lab SOP.
4. Start run and monitor temperature.
5. Pause and cool if sample temperature rises above your SOP limit.
6. Remove samples, then empty/clean bath area as required.

### Care checklist
- Replace bath water on schedule.
- Dry external surfaces after use.
- Do not run device dry or with unbalanced loads.

---

## General Good Habits for All Equipment

- Label every sample clearly before approaching an instrument.
- Keep food, drinks, and personal items away from equipment benches.
- Record instrument use and issues in the lab log.
- If you are unsure, stop and ask your TA or instructor before continuing.
