Active Research Themes

Our teaching environment is shaped by real research questions spanning microbiology, genomics, and host-associated systems.

  • 🐛 Wax Worm Microbiomes & Genomics

    Exploring how wax worm-associated microbial communities and genomic tools can illuminate host-microbe interactions, adaptation, and experimental model systems.

  • 🍄 Fungal Genomics

    Investigating fungal diversity, genome-level variation, and the biological mechanisms that shape how fungi survive, interact, and evolve.

  • 🔭 Delftia spp. Research

    Studying Delftia species through questions in microbial ecology, genetics, and functional traits — connecting genomic data to laboratory observations.
